# One concept at a time

When answering a conceptual question, explain only one concept per reply, keep it short, and wait until the user confirms they understand before introducing the next one.

**Why:** the user said: "từ từ, đi từng cái thôi, tôi rõ rồi mới đi tiếp, k đi nhiều khái niệm cùng lúc".

**How to apply:**
- When a question involves several concepts, choose the first one the others depend on.
- Explain only that concept. A small numeric example helps.
- End with: "Phần này bạn đã rõ chưa? Rõ rồi thì mình qua **<next concept>**."
- When the user restates their understanding, confirm the parts that are right and correct only the details that are wrong, then move on.
