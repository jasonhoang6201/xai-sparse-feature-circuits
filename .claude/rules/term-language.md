# Term language in Vietnamese text

Applies to chat answers and generated files.

- **Keep in English** the domain terms that have no common Vietnamese equivalent, or whose translation reads strangely: interpretability, interpretable, unit, feature, activation, circuit, SAE, dictionary learning, reconstruction, causal effect, intervention, patching, ablation, residual stream, residual connection, skip connection, gradient, superposition, polysemantic, spurious correlation, subject-verb agreement, relative clause, cluster, and feature names (e.g. "noun number detector").
- **Translate to Vietnamese** the common academic words a Vietnamese reader knows without context: giả thuyết (hypothesis), hành vi (behavior), ngưỡng (threshold), trung bình (mean), xấp xỉ (approximate), xác suất (probability), nhãn (label), giới tính (gender), nghề (profession), chủ ngữ / động từ / danh từ, hướng (direction), chiều (dimension), tham số (parameter).
- **Translate by meaning, not word for word.** The user rejected "không cần người viết giả thuyết trước" and wanted "người nghiên cứu không phải đưa ra giả thuyết từ trước". Write each sentence the way a Vietnamese person would say it: add the subject and connecting words, and rearrange the clause order when the result reads more naturally.
